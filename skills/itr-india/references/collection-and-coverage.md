# Collection and source coverage

Use this before portal entry and when a new source changes the reconciliation.
Keep taxpayer records in the private filing workspace, never in the skill,
fixtures or PR. For salary, use [multiple-form-16.md](multiple-form-16.md);
for account and held-asset checks, use
[assets-and-bank-disclosures.md](assets-and-bank-disclosures.md).

## Adaptive intake, then one collection batch

1. Establish FY/AY, residence, employment dates, income sources, business activity,
   prior losses/elections and whether asset disclosure applies. Read the year
   inside documents; a filename or the user's description can identify an older
   year incorrectly. Check applicable filing dates before comparing regimes.
2. Inventory banks, employers, brokers, registrars, direct investments and
   portfolio managers. Check already received files before requesting anything.
   Ask which accounts/platforms are missing rather than assuming the current
   employer, broker or app covers everything.
3. Select reports to answer specific gaps below. Batch downloads while the
   authorized browser session is active; reconcile locally afterward. Before
   asking for another login, have the next useful portal batch ready.
4. Review coverage and control totals. Request only the missing period, lot,
   account or explanation. Equivalent reliable evidence can close a gap without
   a particular branded statement or support response.

| Evidence | Main purpose | Does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Each employer's Form 16 Parts A/B | Salary, exemptions and employer TDS | Other employers or all non-salary income |
| Current-AY 26AS and AIS/TIS | Credit and reported-income completeness checks | Complete income, correct classification or acquisition cost |
| Bank annual-interest certificate | Annual interest and sometimes accrual | All deposit principal, year-end balances or account identifiers |
| Statement covering year-end | Dated balance, actual credits, exempt accounts | Full-year interest if only a partial period is supplied |
| Broker tax P&L and lot detail | Trading classification, realized gains and fees | Complete outside holdings or year-end original cost |
| Registrar capital-gain report | Disposed units, costs and gain controls | Every folio or remaining holdings |
| Full transaction CAS/history | Coverage, units, missing fund transactions | Missing pre-history cost or every demat holding |
| Portfolio annual accounts and schedules | Investments, coupons, accruals and disposals | Taxpayer's tax method or absence of registrar overlap |
| Prior filed return and detailed computation | Prior inclusions, losses and opening clues | Current balances, unchanged exemptions or current payment credits |
| Paid challan | Payment amount, date, AY and identifying details | Credit in the current return until verified |

Provider menus and report names change. Verify actual content, period and
account/folio coverage rather than preserving a fixed click path. Check whether
an apparently consolidated report omits redeemed or unmapped folios; compare
coverage even when a newer report spans more dates.

## Private source and decision register

Use one current register and computation, with links to original evidence:

- source identity, saved filename, internal FY/AY, dates, accounts/folios,
  summary totals and extraction/visual checks;
- exact source figures, documented adjustments, portal-rounded entries and
  actual saved/exported figures, with the reason for each difference;
- **evidence status:** missing, supported, estimated or unresolved;
- **portal status:** entered, persistence verified, confirmed, internally
  validated, upload-validated, paid, submitted or e-verified;
- material issue, next action and explicit resolution criterion.

Evidence status and portal status are separate. A confirmed schedule can still
contain a provisional estimate. An accepted import can fail to save. A prepared
challan is not a paid receipt. Mark obsolete outputs **superseded inside the
file**, including the reason and current replacement, so an old payable cannot
be reused as a payment instruction. Record the form and verification date.

Preserve originals; identify downloaded content before renaming it. Check that
an asynchronous download finished and is the requested report, not an older
file. Empty text extraction can mean scanned pages. Verify extracted dates,
columns and totals visually where necessary. Keep authorized document passwords
transient; they are not portal login credentials.

## Coverage and overlap controls

For investments, map ISIN/security, folio/account, transaction date, units and
consideration across sources. Record which source supplies each transaction and
why copies are excluded. A portfolio summary can mix incremental holdings and
transactions already present in registrar reports; do not subtract one assumed
scheme overlap from an entire portfolio total. Reconcile aggregated versus split
rows explicitly.

Preserve cash proceeds, gross consideration, original cost, effective or
adjusted cost, eligible historical FMV and charges separately. Reconcile source
summaries and quantity continuity before relying on extraction. A date printed
inside a note is not necessarily a transaction date; wrapped identifiers and
folio changes at page boundaries need explicit checks. Do not turn rounded
printed NAV into purportedly exact cost when better source cost exists.

Numerical reconciliation does not decide legal classification. Use
[income-reconciliation.md](income-reconciliation.md) and
[capital-gains-other-sources.md](capital-gains-other-sources.md) for income and
instrument treatment. Stop extra collection once adequate evidence resolves the
material question. Record immaterial limitations and supported estimates without
inventing missing transactions, income or deductions.
