# Choosing ITR-1/2/3/4 for AY 2026-27

Form eligibility changes between assessment years. Use this matrix only for
**FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27** and re-check the notified form and validation rules
before applying it to another year.

## Fast decision matrix

| Fact pattern | Form normally available |
|---|---|
| Resident individual; total income ≤ ₹50 lakh; salary/pension, up to two house properties, eligible other sources, agricultural income ≤ ₹5,000, and aggregate LTCG u/s 112A ≤ ₹1.25 lakh | ITR-1 |
| Individual/HUF with no business/profession income who cannot use ITR-1 (for example, STCG, 112A LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh, other capital gains, multiple disqualifying sources, or foreign assets/income) | ITR-2 |
| Individual/HUF with business/profession income who cannot use ITR-4 | ITR-3 |
| Resident individual/HUF, or resident firm other than LLP; total income ≤ ₹50 lakh; presumptive income u/s 44AD/44ADA/44AE; permitted salary/pension, up to two house properties and other sources; agricultural income ≤ ₹5,000; aggregate LTCG u/s 112A ≤ ₹1.25 lakh | ITR-4 |

ITR-1 and ITR-4 remain optional simplified forms. Meeting a row is not enough:
check every disqualifier below.

## Capital-gain boundary that commonly changes the answer

For AY 2026-27, a small amount of **long-term capital gain under section 112A
does not automatically rule out ITR-1 or ITR-4**. Aggregate eligible 112A LTCG
up to ₹1.25 lakh is permitted.

The carve-out is narrow:

- any **short-term capital gain**, including section 111A gain, rules out ITR-1
  and ITR-4;
- section 112A LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh rules them out;
- capital gains under other provisions (property, gold, debt/non-equity funds,
  unlisted shares, etc.) require the fuller applicable form; and
- brought-forward or carry-forward losses and the other notified
  disqualifications must still be checked.

Therefore:

- presumptive 44ADA income + ₹75,000 eligible 112A LTCG may fit ITR-4;
- presumptive 44ADA income + ₹1 of listed-equity STCG requires ITR-3; and
- salary + eligible 112A LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh may fit ITR-1 if every other
  condition is met.

## House-property boundary

AY 2026-27 ITR-1 and ITR-4 allow reporting income from **up to two house
properties**, subject to their other conditions. Older one-house summaries
should not be reused for this assessment year.

## High-signal ITR-4 disqualifiers

Do not use ITR-4 merely because income was computed presumptively. Use ITR-3
when any ITR-4 disqualifier applies, including:

- total income above ₹50 lakh;
- RNOR or non-resident status;
- STCG, 112A LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh, or another kind of capital gain;
- more than two house properties;
- director status or holding unlisted equity shares during the year;
- foreign assets, signing authority outside India, or foreign-source income;
- brought-forward loss or loss to be carried forward;
- income taxable at a special rate not supported by the simplified form; or
- any other condition in the notified form or current validation rules.

When in doubt, use the department's “Help me decide which ITR form to file”
wizard as a cross-check, but verify its result against the actual income
sources and the notified rules.
